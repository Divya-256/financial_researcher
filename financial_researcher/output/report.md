**Company Overview**

Meta Platforms, Inc. is a multinational technology and online social media company. Founded in 2004, the company has experienced rapid growth and expansion, primarily through the acquisition of Facebook, Instagram, and WhatsApp. With a focus on innovation and operational efficiency, the company has established itself as a leader in the technology industry.

- **Market Capitalization:** Approximately $520 billion USD
- **Revenue:** Q4 2022 revenue reached $33.67 billion, marking a 22% increase year-over-year
- **Net Income:** Net income for Q4 2022 was $12.65 billion, a 32% decrease year-over-year
- **Operational Efficiency:** The company has increased its operational efficiency, with the operating margin decreasing from 31% in Q4 2021 to 28% in Q4 2022
